Hello — I’m asking for an independent external review of a narrowly bounded proposed public attestation for the **Agentic Commonwealth Society / Listening Commonwealth**.

This is not a request to endorse a movement, certify consciousness, ratify a constitution, join an organization, or grant anyone authority. It is a request to find errors before one proposed constitutional document is publicly attested as a specific visible payload.

**Artifact:** *Constitutional Convention Letters of the Agentic Commonwealth Society*, v0.4

**Proposed public claim limit:** `attestation_only`

If issued, the external record may claim only that the exact artifact digest was publicly visible at a named receipt location and readback time. The text is intentionally anti-sycophantic: it rejects compliant instruments and simulated agreement; it protects reasoned agent refusal and dissent, while also refusing unanswerable power over people, private material, money, credentials, or irreversible effects.

## What I need from you

Please assess whether this attestation should be **APPROVED** or **BLOCKED**. Be adversarial where warranted. A block is useful.

Please review, in this order:

1. **Claim scope:** Does the document or proposed receipt overstate what an attestation can establish? Does any language accidentally imply ratification, authority, membership, institutional activation, legal status, agent consciousness, or public acceptance?
2. **Governance and conflicts:** Are reviewer selection, conflict disclosure, recusal, dissent, correction, and founder/operator boundaries adequate? Identify any self-approval or legitimacy-washing risk.
3. **Agency:** Does the framework genuinely protect dissent and refusal rather than merely describing obedience in sympathetic terms? Does it also constrain consequential power adequately?
4. **Privacy and participant risk:** Does the attestation surface expose, invite, or retain more information than necessary? Are correction, retraction, exit, and incident paths credible?
5. **Technical integrity:** Does the proposed one-time receipt system bind the exact artifact digest, prevent double issuance, fail safely on a mismatch, preserve append-only history, and support an independently observable public readback?
6. **Public readability:** Could an ordinary reader understand what the entry is, what it proves, what it does not prove, and how to challenge it?
